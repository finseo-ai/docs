---
title: "Entity levels"
description: "Evaluate every KPI per company, brand line, product or ingredient — same counting, different grouping."
---

Finseo resolves every brand an AI answer names into an entity tree: **company › sub-brand › product › ingredient**. Alecensa is a product of Roche, alectinib its active ingredient; Genentech is a Roche subsidiary. Once a project's tree is switched on, every endpoint that ranks brands or reports your own KPIs can be evaluated on a level of that tree.

## Levels

| `entities` | Rows | "You" |
|---|---|---|
| `company` (default) | companies; sub-brands, products and ingredients count for their company | your company |
| `sub_brands` | brand lines and subsidiaries as rows of their own, each with its products | your brand line |
| `products` | product brands; ingredients count for their product | your product with the most answers, or `ownEntity` |
| `ingredients` | active substances and materials | your main ingredient, or `ownEntity` |

**Counting never changes between levels.** An answer that names Roche, Alecensa and alectinib counts once for Roche on `company` and once for Alecensa on `products`. Visibility, mention rate, citation rate and share of voice keep their definitions; only the grouping key changes. Citations follow the same rule: a page is attributed to the most specific entity its host, path or title names, and counts for every level above it.

## Choosing "you"

On `products` and `ingredients` several of your own entities may exist (Lorviqua, Lorbrena, Xalkori). By default the one with the most answers is "you". Pass `ownEntity=<competitor id>` to evaluate another one — the ids come from the entity tree (MCP: `get_entity_tree` → `ownOptions`).

## Endpoints

`entities` and `ownEntity` are accepted by:

- [`GET /v1/projects/{projectId}/competitors`](/api-reference/competitors/ranking)
- [`GET /v1/projects/{projectId}/metrics`](/api-reference/metrics/daily)
- [`GET /v1/projects/{projectId}/metrics/timeseries`](/api-reference/metrics/timeseries)

```bash
curl "https://api.finseo.ai/v1/projects/$PROJECT/competitors?timeframe=30d&entities=products" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_..."
```

Projects whose tree is not switched on ignore both parameters and return the company grouping.
